LEO-II v2.3 -- what is new (October 2026)

Instantiates, when it reads a problem, an axiom whose leading universal
quantifier ranges over sets of properties at the problem's own constants of
that type, and finds identical terms faster.  Measured against v2.2, the two
run side by side on one core each: on the 294 problems of the
ontological-argument dataset 225 against 217 at ten seconds and 235 against
221 at sixty; on the 3637 monomorphic TH0 problems of the TPTP library v7.5.0
1894 against 1883 at ten seconds.  None of the 45 non-theorems, no answer that
contradicts the library's record, no Error.

* Instances of axioms over sets of properties.  Ax1Gen of the Goedel variants
  is needed once, at P, the set of the positive properties, to prove that being
  God-like is positive.  Primitive substitution never offers P, and the general
  axiom drowns the first-order prover.  v2.3 replaces such an axiom, in the
  unfiltered slices, by its instances at the problem's constants of that type
  and at the empty set, made from the formula before clause normal form.  A
  proof from instances is a proof; a saturation of them is no countermodel, so
  after a replacement LEO-II never answers CounterSatisfiable.  Turned off with
  --notInstantiateSets (-nis).

* Positions hashed whole.  The table indexing subterms by position used a hash
  that reads only the first ten steps of a position; deep positions shared a
  bucket.  Same answers, faster.

* Builds with GNU make 4 (the dependency file depended on a phony target).
