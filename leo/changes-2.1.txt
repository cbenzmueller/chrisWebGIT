LEO-II v2.1 -- what is new (September 2026)

Proves 217 of the 294 higher-order modal problems of the ontological-argument
dataset at ten seconds on one core, where v2.0 proves 205, and 225 given the
whole machine (E 3.2: 218 and 227).  Runs vary by two or three.

* A short filtered time slice first.  The relevance filter (--relevancefilter 1)
  keeps only axioms that share symbols with the conjecture; as a default it
  loses, as a first slice with a quarter of the budget it wins twelve problems,
  because what it finds it finds in about a second.  For problems with long
  definitions the schedule now opens with that slice; the unfiltered search
  gets the rest and whatever the slice leaves unused.  Neutral on 400 TPTP
  theorems, nothing proved on 470 TPTP non-theorems.
* Questions are answered.  A formula of role "question" is proved like a
  conjecture and the instances of its existential variables are reported:
      % SZS answers Tuple [[a]]
  The instance comes from the first-order prover, which is handed the
  question's clauses as a question of its own and run with --answers.
* --proofoutput 2 finds epclextract beside eprover when .leoatprc does not
  name it, so the first-order steps of a proof are expanded.
* A stray debug print in directory mode is a warning on level 1.

Details and the measurements are in CHANGES inside the archive.

The 294 problems are the dataset of Benzmüller and Scott's "Notes on Gödel's
and Scott's variants of the ontological argument" (Monatshefte für Mathematik
208, 2025), rendered in TPTP THF.  They travel, with the results of six
provers, as ancillary material of

    C. Benzmüller, Gödel's and Scott's Variants of the Ontological Argument
    in Lean 4 and TPTP THF, arXiv (forthcoming; the link goes here once the
    paper is announced).
